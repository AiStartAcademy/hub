# AI Tools, Data, and Intellectual Property

This program is hands-on. You will connect real accounts, feed real data into third-party AI tools, and build working automations — sometimes against your own business, sometimes against a classmate's. This document sets out who owns what and what you agree not to do with data that is not yours.

## 1. You own what you build

Anything you create during the program — agents, workflows, prompts, automations, code, business plans, client deliverables — is **yours**. {{brand.name}} claims no ownership of it and no license to it beyond what is needed to run the program (displaying your submission to your instructors and cohort, and showing it in class).

You may use what you build commercially, including in an agency you launch afterward. You do not owe us revenue share, and you do not need our permission.

## 2. What stays ours

Course materials, curriculum, workbooks, slide decks, our prompt libraries, our templates, the Thesaurus, and session recordings remain {{brand.name}}'s property, under the license in the Terms of Service.

The line is: **use our templates to build your own thing, yes. Repackage our program as your program, no.** You may not use our materials to create or deliver a competing course, bootcamp, cohort, or training product, and you may not resell or redistribute them.

If a template we gave you is embedded in a deliverable you build for your own client, that is fine and expected.

## 3. Third-party AI tools and accounts

The program uses third-party services — model providers, automation platforms, voice and telephony providers, hosting, and others. You agree that:

- You will create and use your own accounts, in your own name, under those providers' terms.
- Any usage cost, subscription, or overage on your own accounts is yours.
- Those providers' privacy and data-retention terms govern anything you send them. We are not a party to that and cannot control it.
- We are not responsible for their availability, pricing changes, output quality, or for a provider suspending your account.
- You will not use shared {{brand.name}} credentials or API keys outside of a supervised exercise, and you will not extract, copy, or reuse them afterward.

## 4. What you put into AI tools

You are responsible for the data you paste into these tools. Before you paste something, check that you have the right to.

You agree not to submit to any AI tool during this program:

- Another student's confidential business information.
- Personal data belonging to third parties — customer lists, employee records, health information, financial account numbers, government ID numbers — unless you have a lawful basis and the provider's terms permit it.
- Credentials, API keys, or secrets belonging to someone else.
- Material you are contractually forbidden to disclose, including under an employer's or client's NDA.

If you are under an NDA with an employer or client, do not use their data in class exercises. Use your own, or use the sample data we provide.

## 5. Other students' data and ideas

You will see other people's businesses up close. You agree to treat what you see as confidential: do not repost it, do not repeat it publicly, do not use it as training data, and do not build a competing product from an idea a classmate shared in the room.

If you and a classmate collaborate on something and want to build it together afterward, that is between the two of you — agree on ownership in writing before you start. {{brand.name}} is not a party to it and will not arbitrate it.

## 6. AI output

AI output can be wrong, biased, or fabricated, and can inadvertently resemble existing work. You are responsible for reviewing anything you ship, including anything you deliver to a paying client. We make no warranty that AI output produced during the program is accurate, original, non-infringing, or fit for any purpose.

Ownership of AI output is unsettled law in some jurisdictions and varies by provider. We are not giving you legal advice about it.

## 7. Client work

If you use what you learn here to serve your own clients, that relationship is entirely yours. You are responsible for your own contracts, deliverables, data handling, and compliance. Do not present {{brand.name}} as a party to, a guarantor of, or a partner in your client work, and do not use our name or logo in your own marketing without written permission.
