# Recording and Media Release

Every {{brand.name}} session is recorded. This document explains what we capture, what we do with it, and what you can decline.

There are two separate agreements here. **Part A is required** to take part in a cohort. **Part B is optional** and you can decline it without any effect on your enrollment.

## What we record

Sessions are recorded on Zoom and, for in-person sessions, on cameras and microphones in the room at {{brand.address.street}}. A recording can include your image, your voice, your name and email as displayed, your screen when you share it, what you present, questions you ask, and the session chat.

We also produce derivative material from recordings: transcripts, captions, summaries, clips, and course materials.

---

## Part A — Consent to record (required)

By accepting Part A, you agree that:

1. {{brand.name}} may record the sessions of your cohort, including your participation in them, and may produce transcripts, captions, and summaries from those recordings.
2. We may make the recordings and derivatives available to the students, instructors, and staff of your cohort, and to students of later cohorts of the same or a related program, inside the Hub.
3. We may use recordings internally for teaching, quality review, curriculum development, and record-keeping.
4. You will not receive payment for this, and you are not entitled to approve, review, or receive a copy of a recording before it is used in the ways described above.

We do this because the recording *is* part of the product — students who miss a session catch up from it, and the whole cohort revisits it later. There is no way to run the program without it.

**If you decline Part A, you cannot participate.** Contact {{brand.email.contact}} and we will transfer or refund you.

### Your control during a session

You may turn off your camera. You may ask a question with your camera off. If you need to discuss something you do not want captured, ask the instructor and we will take it off recording or handle it after the session. If something sensitive is captured by accident, email {{brand.email.contact}} and we will cut it.

## Part B — Promotional use of your likeness (optional)

This is the part you can say no to.

If you accept Part B, you grant {{brand.name}} a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use your image, voice, likeness, name, and statements captured during the program in **public** marketing and promotional material — including social media, YouTube, our website, ads, event pages, and course previews — without further approval or compensation. You waive any right to inspect or approve the finished material.

If you decline Part B:

- We will not knowingly feature you in public promotional material.
- We will not publish a clip in which you are the subject.
- You may still appear incidentally — for example, briefly in the background of a wide shot of the room, or your voice in a question during a segment that is published. If you spot something you want removed, email {{brand.email.contact}} and we will remove or blur it.
- Nothing about your participation, grading, seat, or access changes.

**Testimonials are always separate.** If we want to use a testimonial, a case study, your company name, or your results, we will ask you specifically at that time. Accepting Part B is not permission for that.

### Withdrawing Part B

You can withdraw at any time by emailing {{brand.email.contact}}. We will stop using your likeness in new material and will take down published material where we reasonably can. We cannot recall material already distributed by third parties, already printed, or embedded in an ad campaign that has run, and we are not required to re-edit a finished long-form video, though we will where it is practical.

## Other people's material

Recordings may include other students' screens, businesses, and ideas. Your obligations to them are in the Community and Acceptable Use Policy — in short, do not repost, and do not repeat what was shared in the room.
