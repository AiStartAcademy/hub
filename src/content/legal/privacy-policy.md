# {{brand.name}} Privacy Policy

{{brand.legalName}} ("{{brand.shortName}}", "we", "us"), located at {{brand.legalAddress}}, operates {{brand.domains.hub}} and {{brand.domains.site}} (the "Service"). This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information.

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** name, email address, password, and timezone.
- **Profile information:** profile image, bio, headline, location, languages, links, and expertise tags, where you choose to add them.
- **Onboarding and lead forms:** your answers, which may include phone number and details about your work and goals.
- **Enrollment and payment information:** the cohort you bought, the seat tier, buyer name and email, and the payment status. Card details go directly to our payment processor (Stripe) and are never stored by us.
- **Coursework:** assessment and quiz submissions, scores, lesson progress, and reviews.
- **Communications:** anything you send us for support, scheduling, or scholarship requests.
- **Consent records:** which policies you accepted, at which version, when, and from which IP address and browser.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Log data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, referral URLs, pages viewed, and access times.
- **Cookies:** essential cookies for authentication and session management, and functional cookies for preferences such as theme and timezone.
- **Marketing attribution:** UTM parameters and ad click identifiers captured when you first arrive, stored first-touch against your lead or account so we know which campaign brought you.

### 1.3 Session recordings

Cohort sessions are recorded. Recordings include video, audio, screen shares, and chat from the session, and may include your image, voice, name, and anything you present or say. How recordings are used, and your options, are set out in the Recording and Media Release.

### 1.4 Information from third parties

- **Google OAuth:** if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
- **Payment processor:** Stripe sends us the outcome of your payment, the last four digits and brand of the card, and billing email.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Create and manage your account and verify who you are.
- Operate the learning platform, enroll you in cohorts and programs, and track your progress.
- Process payments, memberships, refunds, and seat transfers.
- Send transactional email: account setup, cohort updates, session reminders, and receipts.
- Produce, caption, and distribute session recordings and course content, subject to the Recording and Media Release.
- Improve the Service and our teaching, including analyzing which lessons and formats work.
- Measure marketing performance and attribute enrollments to campaigns, including sending hashed identifiers to advertising and analytics platforms where you have not opted out.
- Generate course and cohort recommendations from your onboarding answers, using a third-party AI model provider.
- Respond to your questions and provide support.
- Comply with law, enforce our Terms, and prevent fraud and abuse.

## 3. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

- **Contract performance:** to deliver the program you enrolled in.
- **Legitimate interests:** to improve the Service, secure it, and prevent fraud.
- **Consent:** for optional processing such as promotional use of your likeness and non-essential marketing email.
- **Legal obligations:** to meet tax, accounting, and other legal requirements.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

- **Service providers:** payment processing (Stripe), email delivery, cloud hosting and storage, video conferencing and recording (Zoom), community chat, CRM, and AI model providers used to process onboarding responses. Providers act on our instructions.
- **Instructors and program administrators:** your enrollment, attendance, submissions, and onboarding answers are visible to the instructors and administrators of cohorts you are enrolled in.
- **Other participants:** your name, profile, and anything you say or share in a session or community space is visible to your cohort. Session recordings shared with the cohort include you.
- **Advertising and analytics platforms:** aggregate and event-level conversion data, which may include a hashed email address, so we can measure campaigns. You can ask us to stop this by emailing {{brand.email.privacy}}.
- **Legal requirements:** where required by law, subpoena, court order, or government request.
- **Business transfers:** in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your account information while your account is active. Enrollment, payment, and consent records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax, and legal defense. Session recordings are kept for as long as we make them available to cohorts and for archival purposes; we may remove them at any time. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

## 6. Cookies

Essential cookies are required for sign-in and cannot be disabled. Functional cookies remember your preferences. Advertising and analytics identifiers are used on our public marketing pages; you can control these through your browser and through your ad-platform settings.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

**Everyone.** You can access and update your account information in your settings, request deletion by emailing {{brand.email.privacy}}, and unsubscribe from non-essential email using the link in any such message. You can withdraw your consent to promotional use of your likeness at any time; see the Recording and Media Release for what that does and does not undo.

**California residents (CCPA/CPRA).** You have the right to know what we have collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information. To exercise these rights, email {{brand.email.privacy}}; we will verify your identity first.

**Other US states.** Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other states with applicable privacy laws have similar rights. Contact us at the same address.

## 8. Data Security

We hash passwords with bcrypt, serve all traffic over HTTPS, use secure session management, restrict access to authorized personnel, and serve course media through short-lived signed URLs. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are between 13 and 18, use the Service only with a parent or guardian involved. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email {{brand.email.privacy}}.

## 10. International Users

We operate in the United States and process information there. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information is transferred to and processed in the United States.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes are published here with a new version, and we will ask you to accept the updated version the next time you access course content. We record which version you accepted and when.
