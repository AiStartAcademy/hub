# Community and Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies everywhere {{brand.name}} brings people together: cohort sessions in person and on Zoom, the Hub, chat and community spaces, group projects, office hours, and the venue at {{brand.address.street}}.

You are in a room with other founders and operators who are sharing real businesses, real numbers, and half-finished ideas. That only works if the room is safe. This policy is what makes it safe.

## 1. Be a good participant

- Treat everyone — students, instructors, staff, guests, and venue neighbors — with respect.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Personal attacks, name-calling, and pile-ons are not acceptable.
- Keep discussion relevant to the session or channel you are in.
- Let other people finish. Share airtime.
- Follow instructions from instructors and staff, especially about safety and the venue.

## 2. Not allowed

- Harassment, bullying, stalking, intimidation, or threats, in any space or by direct message.
- Slurs or demeaning commentary based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, age, appearance, or immigration status.
- Unwanted sexual attention, sexual content, or sexualized imagery.
- Content that is defamatory, obscene, or otherwise objectionable in a professional learning environment.
- Doxxing or posting anyone's private information without permission.
- Impersonating another person, instructor, or {{brand.name}} itself.
- Spam, unsolicited pitching, multi-level marketing recruitment, or scraping the cohort roster to build a lead list.
- Sharing your account, seat, session links, or paid materials with people who did not enroll.
- Deliberately disrupting a session, the Hub, or the venue.
- Anything illegal, or any use of what you learn here to build tools for harassment, fraud, unauthorized access to systems you do not own, or mass deceptive content.

## 3. Confidentiality of the room

What other students share about their business — revenue, customers, strategy, unlaunched ideas — stays in the room. Do not repeat it publicly, post it, or build on it without that person's permission. This is a mutual obligation and it is the reason people can be candid here.

You may always share your own work, your own results, and general lessons from the program.

## 4. Recording and posting

Only {{brand.name}} records sessions. Do not make your own recordings, screen captures, or photos of sessions, other students, or other students' screens without permission from staff and from the people in frame.

Do not post course materials, recordings, transcripts, or other students' work publicly.

## 5. Content you post

You are responsible for what you post in the Hub, in chat, and in shared projects. You keep ownership of it, and you grant {{brand.name}} permission to host, display, and distribute it within the program for the purpose of running the program.

We may remove content that violates this policy, and we do not pre-screen or endorse content posted by participants.

## 6. In-person conduct

At the venue: no weapons, no illegal substances, and no disruptive intoxication. Do not damage the space or other people's equipment. Do not let non-participants into the building. Respect the building's other tenants and neighbors, particularly in the evenings.

## 7. Reporting

If something happens — to you or to someone else — tell an instructor on site or email {{brand.email.contact}}. Reports are handled discreetly. We will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith, and we ask that you do not either.

## 8. Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may give a warning, remove content, mute or remove you from a session or channel, remove you from the venue, or terminate your enrollment. Serious violations — threats, violence, harassment, or endangering others — can result in immediate removal without refund and, where appropriate, a report to law enforcement.

We aim to be proportionate and to talk to you first, except where safety requires acting immediately.
